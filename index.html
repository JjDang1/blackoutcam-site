BlackoutCam

A discreet iPhone camera you control with the volume buttons.

How it works

  • Volume up starts and stops recording (or takes a photo).
  • Volume down flips between the front and back camera. You can turn this off.
  • Tap the moon to make the screen go dark. The camera keeps running.
  • On the dark screen, tap once to check: a long buzz means recording, a light tick means not recording.
  • To leave the dark screen, hold anywhere for 1 second. You can change this to a triple-tap or pressing both volume buttons.
  • Tap any ⓘ in the app for an explanation of that setting.

Common questions

Why did my recording stop? iOS only lets apps use the camera while they're open. Locking the phone or leaving the app stops the recording and saves it.

Where are my recordings? Videos and photos go to the Photos app. Audio recordings go to Files → On My iPhone → BlackoutCam. If "Save to private gallery" is on, they stay inside the app.

Why is there a green dot at the top of the screen? iOS always shows this when an app uses the camera, and apps can't hide it.

How do I use the Apple Watch remote? Open BlackoutCam on your iPhone first, then open it on your watch.

Please record responsibly

Recording people without their consent is illegal in many places. Check your local laws, and don't record people where they expect privacy.
