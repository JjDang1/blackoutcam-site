Privacy Policy

BlackoutCam · Effective September 29, 2026

BlackoutCam does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer never receives your photos, videos, audio, or any other data.

Camera and microphone

BlackoutCam uses the camera and microphone only to make the photos, videos, and audio recordings you ask for. Recording happens entirely on your iPhone.

Where your recordings are stored

  • Photos: videos and photos are saved to your Photos library. BlackoutCam only asks for permission to add items, not to read your library.
  • Files: audio recordings are saved in the app's folder in the Files app.
  • Private gallery: if you turn it on, recordings are kept inside the app on your iPhone.

Anything you save may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, according to your iPhone settings. BlackoutCam itself does not upload anything.

Face ID

Face ID (or your passcode) can lock the private gallery. This is handled entirely by iOS. BlackoutCam never sees or stores your face data or passcode.

Photos you pick for the dark screen

If you choose a photo for the "Your photo" or "Lock screen" look, a copy is stored inside the app on your iPhone and is used only to show that screen.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch remote sends commands and recording status directly between your watch and iPhone. Nothing is sent to any server.

Children

BlackoutCam does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
